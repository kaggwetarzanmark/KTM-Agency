## What is Boosting a Post? {#what-is-boosting}

Boosting is the simplest form of Facebook advertising. You click "Boost Post" on an existing post, set a budget, choose a broad audience and Facebook shows that post to more people.

It takes about 2 minutes to set up and requires no technical knowledge.

**What boosting can do:**
- Increase the reach of a post (show it to more people)
- Get more likes, comments and shares
- Drive some traffic to your website

**What boosting cannot do:**
- Optimise for lead generation
- Use advanced audience targeting
- Run split tests between different creatives
- Track conversions or sales properly
- Access most campaign objectives (leads, conversions, messages)
- Create Click-to-WhatsApp ads

<div class="warn">
<strong>⚠️ Warning:</strong> Boosting a post is not the same as running a Facebook Ads campaign. If your goal is leads, phone calls or WhatsApp enquiries, boosting will almost always disappoint. You are paying for reach and engagement, not for customers.
</div>

## What are Proper Facebook Ads? {#what-are-facebook-ads}

Facebook Ads (also called Meta Ads) are campaigns created through **Ads Manager**, Facebook's full advertising platform. Ads Manager gives you access to every feature Facebook offers.

This includes:
- All campaign objectives (leads, sales, traffic, awareness, messages, WhatsApp)
- Detailed audience targeting (custom audiences, lookalike audiences, interest targeting)
- Placement control (Facebook, Instagram, Stories, Reels, Messenger)
- A/B testing
- Conversion tracking via the Meta Pixel
- Retargeting website visitors
- Detailed analytics by audience, placement and creative

The learning curve is steeper than clicking "Boost," but the results are significantly better, especially in Uganda where Click-to-WhatsApp ads are among the highest-converting ad formats available.

## Why Boosting Rarely Works for Uganda Businesses {#why-boosting-fails}

### You cannot optimise for leads

The most valuable action for most Uganda businesses is getting a phone number or WhatsApp contact from a potential customer. Boosting cannot optimise for this. Ads Manager has a dedicated **Leads** objective with a native lead form that collects contact information directly on Facebook without the customer needing to leave the platform.

### The targeting is too shallow

Boosting gives you basic targeting: location, age range and a few broad interest categories. Ads Manager lets you target:
- People who have visited your website in the last 30 days
- People who look like your existing customers (lookalike audiences)
- People with very specific interests and behaviours
- Your existing customer list matched by phone number or email

This difference in targeting quality directly affects how much you pay per lead.

### No WhatsApp integration

In Uganda, WhatsApp is how business happens. Ads Manager lets you create Click-to-WhatsApp ads, the user clicks your ad and is immediately taken to a WhatsApp conversation with your business, with a pre-filled opening message. This is one of the highest-converting ad formats in the Ugandan market. Boosting does not offer this.

### You cannot retarget

If someone visits your website after seeing your ad but does not contact you, a boosted post cannot follow up. Ads Manager, combined with the Meta Pixel on your website, lets you retarget those visitors with a second ad. Retargeting audiences convert 2 to 5 times better than cold audiences because the person already knows your business.

### No meaningful analytics

Boosting shows you reach, likes and link clicks. Ads Manager shows you cost per lead, cost per conversion, return on ad spend and which specific audience segments and creatives are performing best. Without this data you cannot improve your results over time.

## When Boosting is Acceptable {#when-boosting-ok}

Boosting is not completely useless. It is appropriate when:

- You want to increase organic reach on a post that is already performing well
- You are promoting a time-sensitive event and want quick visibility
- You have no website and no Pixel set up yet
- You are testing whether a Facebook audience responds to a new message before building a full campaign

For these narrow use cases, boosting is fine. For anything involving lead generation, sales or building a real customer pipeline, use Ads Manager.

## How to Get Started with Ads Manager {#getting-started}

1. Go to [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com)
2. Create a Business Manager account
3. Connect your Facebook Page
4. Create an Ad Account (set currency to UGX, timezone to Kampala EAT)
5. Install the Meta Pixel on your website
6. Create your first campaign using the Leads or Messages objective
7. Set your audience to Uganda and your relevant age range

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faqs}

**Is boosting ever worth the money in Uganda?**

For pure awareness and reach, occasionally yes. For lead generation and sales, almost never. The targeting and optimisation limitations make boosting significantly less cost-effective than Ads Manager for commercial goals.

**How much should I spend on Facebook Ads Manager per month in Uganda?**

A realistic starting budget is UGX 200,000 to 400,000 per month in ad spend, plus a management fee if you hire an agency. This generates enough data to optimise properly. Read our [Facebook Ads guide](/blog/facebook-ads-guide-uganda/) for detailed budget guidance.

**Do I need a website to run Facebook Ads in Uganda?**

Not necessarily. The Leads objective collects contact information directly on Facebook without sending traffic to a website. However, having a website dramatically increases what you can do, especially retargeting and conversion tracking. See [why Uganda businesses need a website](/blog/why-every-business-in-uganda-needs-a-website/) for more context.

**What is the Meta Pixel and do I need it?**

The Meta Pixel is a snippet of code added to your website that tracks visitor behaviour. It powers retargeting, lookalike audiences and conversion tracking. Without it, your ads are significantly less powerful. Yes, you need it.
