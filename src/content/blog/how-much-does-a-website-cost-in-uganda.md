If you're a business owner in Uganda looking to get a website, one of the first questions you'll ask is: **how much does a website cost in Uganda?**

The honest answer is that prices vary widely — from UGX 500,000 for a basic template site to UGX 20,000,000+ for a custom e-commerce platform. This guide breaks down what drives the cost and what you should expect to pay for each type of site.

## What Affects the Cost of a Website in Uganda?

Before looking at price ranges, it helps to understand what you're actually paying for:

- **Design complexity** — a custom design costs more than a template
- **Number of pages** — more pages means more work
- **Functionality** — a booking system or payment integration adds significant cost
- **Content** — does the agency write your text and take your photos, or do you provide them?
- **SEO setup** — a site built with SEO costs more upfront but saves money long-term
- **Maintenance** — ongoing support after launch

## Website Price Ranges in Uganda

### Basic Business Website — UGX 1.5M to 3M

This covers a 3 to 5 page site with:
- Home, About, Services, Contact pages
- Mobile-friendly design
- Contact form and WhatsApp button
- Basic SEO setup
- Google Maps embed

This is the right starting point for a new business that just needs a professional online presence. Most small businesses in Kampala fall into this category.

### Standard Business Website — UGX 3M to 7M

For businesses that need more:
- Up to 10 pages
- Blog section
- Gallery or portfolio
- Advanced SEO setup with keyword research
- Google Analytics integration
- 3 rounds of revisions

This is the most common package for established businesses that want to rank on Google and generate enquiries.

### E-Commerce Website — UGX 7M to 20M

Online stores require significantly more work:
- Product catalogue setup
- MTN MoMo and Airtel Money integration
- Order management system
- Customer account portal
- Inventory tracking
- Security and SSL setup

The price depends heavily on the number of products and the payment methods required.

### Custom Web Application — UGX 15M+

If you need something that doesn't fit a standard website — a booking platform, a delivery management system, a membership portal — you're looking at custom development. These projects are scoped individually.

## What's Usually Included in Uganda Web Design Packages

Most reputable agencies in Kampala include:

- Domain and hosting setup (check if it's free for year one or charged separately)
- SSL certificate (HTTPS)
- Mobile-responsive design
- Basic on-page SEO
- WhatsApp chat button

Watch out for agencies that quote a low price but charge separately for hosting, SSL and mobile optimisation — these are essential, not extras.

## What Drives Prices Up

1. **E-commerce and payments** — integrating MoMo, Airtel Money and cards requires developer time and testing
2. **Custom design** — a design built specifically for your brand takes more time than dropping your content into a template
3. **Content creation** — if you need the agency to write your pages and take product photos, that adds cost
4. **Multilingual support** — building a site in English and Luganda doubles the content work
5. **Speed and rush delivery** — needing a site in 3 days instead of 3 weeks costs more

## Red Flags When Comparing Quotes

- **No written contract** — if there's no agreement outlining what's included, you have no protection
- **No maintenance plan** — who fixes it when something breaks after launch?
- **No SEO included** — a site that can't be found on Google isn't doing its job
- **Very cheap hosting** — slow hosting on shared servers common in Uganda hurts your Google ranking
- **No portfolio** — if you can't see examples of their work, you don't know what you're buying

## How to Get the Most Value

1. **Provide your content upfront** — have your text, logo and photos ready before the project starts to avoid delays and extra charges
2. **Be clear about your goals** — a site for brand credibility needs different things than a site designed to generate daily leads
3. **Ask about SEO** — ask specifically which keywords the agency will target and how they'll set up your Google Business Profile
4. **Think long-term** — a UGX 2M site that ranks on Google and brings 20 enquiries a month is better value than a UGX 500K site that nobody finds
