## What Affects the Cost of a Website? {#what-affects-cost}

Before looking at price ranges, understand what you are actually paying for. Two websites can look similar on the surface and cost ten times differently based on what is underneath.

| Factor | Low-cost impact | High-cost impact |
|--------|----------------|-----------------|
| Design | Generic template | Custom design built to your brand |
| Number of pages | 3 to 5 pages | 20+ pages with unique layouts |
| Functionality | Contact form only | Booking system, payments, member portal |
| SEO setup | Basic title tags | Full keyword research, on-page SEO, schema |
| Content | You provide everything | Agency writes and photographs it all |
| Hosting | Cheap shared server | Fast global CDN (Cloudflare, Vercel) |
| Maintenance | None after launch | Ongoing monthly support |
| Mobile optimisation | Responsive template | Tested on actual Uganda Android devices |

<strong>💡 Expert Tip from KTM Agency:</strong> The biggest hidden cost in Uganda web design is SEO. A site that is not built with SEO foundations from day one will require expensive rework later, or simply never be found on Google. Always ask specifically what SEO work is included before accepting a quote.

## Website Price Ranges in Uganda {#price-ranges}

### Basic Business Website, UGX 500,000 to 1,000,000

What you get:
- 3 to 5 pages (Home, About, Services, Contact)
- A standard template design
- Contact form and WhatsApp button
- Mobile-friendly layout
- Basic Google Maps embed

What you do not get:
- Custom design
- SEO keyword research or optimisation
- Fast hosting
- Ongoing support
- Blog or content management

This is appropriate for a very new business that simply needs to exist online. It is not appropriate for a business that wants to rank on Google or generate regular leads.

### Standard Business Website, UGX 1,350,000 to 2,500,000

What you get:
- 6 to 10 custom pages
- Mobile-first design tested on Uganda Android devices
- SEO setup including keyword research, title tags, meta descriptions
- Blog section to publish content
- Google Analytics and Search Console setup
- Gallery or portfolio section
- 3 to 5 rounds of revisions

This is the most common range for established Kampala businesses that want to rank on Google and generate consistent enquiries.

### E-Commerce Website, UGX 2,250,000 to 5,000,000

What you get:
- Full product catalogue
- MTN MoMo and Airtel Money integration
- Card payment processing (Pesapal or similar)
- Order management system
- Customer account portal
- Inventory tracking
- Security and SSL setup

The price depends heavily on the number of products and the payment methods required. A 20-product fashion store costs less than a 500-product hardware shop.

### Custom Web Application, UGX 5,000,000+

Custom web applications, booking platforms, delivery management systems, membership portals, multi-vendor marketplaces, are scoped and priced individually after a detailed discovery session. These are not templated and require significant backend development.

## What Drives Prices Up? {#what-drives-price-up}

Understanding these factors helps you scope your project accurately and avoid budget surprises:

**1. E-commerce and payment integration**
MTN MoMo and Airtel Money integration requires developer time, API setup and testing across different network conditions. Add UGX 500K to 1.5M depending on complexity.

**2. Custom design (not a template)**
A design built from scratch for your brand takes 3 to 5 times longer than placing your content in a template. This is worth the investment if brand differentiation matters to your business.

**3. Content creation**
If you need the agency to write your page copy and photograph your products or premises, add UGX 300K to 1M depending on volume.

**4. Multiple languages**
Building a site in both English and Luganda (or Swahili) roughly doubles the content work.

**5. Speed and rush delivery**
Needing a site in 5 days instead of 3 weeks commands a premium of 25 to 50%.

**6. CMS training and handover**
If you want to update the site yourself without calling a developer, factor in time for proper training and documentation.

## Hidden Costs to Watch Out For {#hidden-costs}

Many Uganda website quotes look attractive but exclude costs that materialise later:

| Hidden cost | Typical range | How to avoid it |
|------------|--------------|----------------|
| Annual domain renewal | UGX 80K to 200K/year | Ask who owns the domain and what renewal costs |
| Annual hosting fees | UGX 200K to 800K/year | Ask specifically where the site is hosted and the annual cost |
| SSL certificate renewal | UGX 50K to 300K/year | Ask if SSL is included and if it auto-renews |
| Content updates after launch | UGX 50K to 150K per change | Ask if any updates are included post-launch |
| Plugin or theme licence renewals | UGX 100K to 500K/year | Ask if any paid plugins or themes are used |
| Email setup | UGX 100K to 300K/year | Ask if business email (you@yourdomain.com) is included |

<div class="warn">
<strong>⚠️ Warning:</strong> Some Ugandan agencies register the domain in their own name, not yours. If you leave them later, you can lose your domain. Always insist that the domain is registered in your business name or your personal Google account.
</div>

## How to Get the Most Value {#get-value}

Follow these steps to maximise your investment:

**1. Provide your content upfront**
Have your text, logo, photos and brand colours ready before the project starts. Delays from missing content are the most common cause of projects going over budget.

**2. Be clear about your goals**
A website for brand credibility needs different things than a website designed to generate daily leads. Communicate your primary goal clearly.

**3. Ask specifically about SEO**
Request a list of the exact keywords they will target, how they will set up your Google Business Profile and what on-page SEO work is included.

**4. Think 3 years, not 3 months**
The right website pays for itself in new business within weeks. Think about what 10 new customers per month from Google would be worth to your business over 3 years, then evaluate the investment accordingly.

**5. Get three comparable quotes**
Ensure each quote covers the same scope. A comparison checklist (pages, SEO, hosting, support, SSL) makes this straightforward.

<div class="checklist">
<h4>✅ Quote Comparison Checklist</h4>

- Same number of pages quoted by each agency
- SEO setup explicitly included and described
- Hosting location and annual cost stated
- SSL certificate included
- Domain registered in your name
- Post-launch support period stated
- Who owns the code after completion
- Payment terms: percentage upfront and on completion
- Timeline from start to launch

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faqs}

**How long does a website take to build in Uganda?**

A standard 6 to 10 page business website takes 2 to 4 weeks from the discovery call to launch, assuming you provide content promptly. E-commerce sites typically take 4 to 8 weeks. Custom applications take longer.

**Can I get a website for under UGX 500,000?**

You can, but with significant compromises, usually a template, no SEO, cheap hosting and no support. For most businesses, this creates more problems than it solves within 12 months.

**Do I need to pay for hosting every year?**

With most agencies, yes, hosting is an annual cost. At KTM, we host on Cloudflare Pages which has a free tier sufficient for most business websites, reducing or eliminating ongoing hosting fees.

**Can I update the website myself after it is built?**

It depends on how it is built. KTM can set up a simple content management system so you can update prices, photos and text without calling a developer. Ask about this when requesting a quote.

**What if I already have a website and just want it improved?**

Yes, this is often more cost-effective than rebuilding from scratch. We audit your existing site, identify what is holding it back from ranking and recommend the specific fixes needed.
