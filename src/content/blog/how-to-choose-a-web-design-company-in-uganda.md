## 1. Look at Their Portfolio First {#portfolio}

The single most important thing to check is their previous work. Any credible web design company in Uganda should be able to show you:

- At least 5 completed websites with live URLs
- Work across different industries
- Sites you can test on your actual phone

When reviewing their portfolio, ask yourself:

- Do the sites load quickly on mobile data?
- Do they look modern and professional?
- Is there a clear call-to-action on each page?
- Can I find these businesses on Google?

<div class="warn">
<strong>⚠️ Warning:</strong> Some agencies show design mockups or screenshots instead of live URLs. Always insist on visiting the actual live website. A mockup looks good — a slow, broken live site tells you the real story.
</div>

## 2. Check If They Understand SEO {#seo}

A beautiful website that nobody can find on Google is a wasted investment. Before hiring any web design company in Uganda, ask these questions directly:

- "Do you do keyword research before building the site?"
- "How do you handle on-page SEO — title tags, meta descriptions, headings?"
- "Will you set up my Google Business Profile?"
- "Will the site appear in Google Search Console after launch?"

A good agency will give you specific, concrete answers. A poor one will say "we make your site SEO-friendly" without being able to explain what that actually means.

<div class="tip">
<strong>💡 Expert Tip from KTM Agency:</strong> Ask to see the Google Search Console data from one of their existing client websites. If they have done proper SEO work, they will have access to it and will be happy to show you. If they cannot show you ranking data, their SEO work is likely superficial.
</div>

## 3. Understand What Is Included {#whats-included}

Many Uganda web design quotes look cheap until you see what they exclude. Before comparing prices, get clarity on every line item:

| Item | Should it be included? | What to ask |
|------|----------------------|-------------|
| Domain registration | Yes — for year one at minimum | Who registers it — you or the agency? |
| Hosting setup | Yes | Where is it hosted? What is the annual cost? |
| SSL certificate | Yes | Is it included and does it auto-renew? |
| Mobile responsive design | Yes — non-negotiable | Will you test on actual Android devices? |
| SEO setup | Yes | Which keywords will you target? |
| Content upload | Clarify | Do you upload my content or do I? |
| Training | Important | Will you train me to update the site myself? |
| Post-launch support | Important | What happens when something breaks after launch? |

<div class="warn">
<strong>⚠️ Warning:</strong> Some agencies register your domain in their own name. If you leave them later, you can lose your domain and your Google ranking history along with it. Always insist the domain is registered in your business name or personal Google account.
</div>

## 4. Ask About Their Process {#process}

A professional web design company has a defined process. Ask them to walk you through it:

1. How do they start a project? (discovery call, brief, questionnaire?)
2. How do they handle revisions — how many rounds are included?
3. What do they need from you and when?
4. What is the timeline from first call to launch?
5. Who is your main point of contact throughout?

Vague answers signal a disorganised operation that will frustrate you throughout the project.

## 5. Check Their Communication {#communication}

How quickly do they respond to your first enquiry? This is a strong predictor of how they will communicate during the project and after launch.

In Uganda's market, WhatsApp responsiveness is a key indicator. A team that takes 3 days to reply to a sales enquiry will take longer to fix a post-launch problem. Test them before you hire them.

## 6. Ask About Hosting {#hosting}

Where your website is hosted has a major impact on speed — and speed is a Google ranking factor and a key conversion factor in Uganda where many users are on mobile data.

Ask:

- "Where will my website be hosted?"
- "What is the uptime guarantee?"
- "How fast does it load on an MTN or Airtel 4G connection?"

The best agencies in Uganda host on Cloudflare Pages, Vercel or similar global CDNs. A site hosted on a cheap local shared server will load slowly and rank poorly.

## 7. Understand the Maintenance Arrangement {#maintenance}

Websites need ongoing attention — security updates, content changes, bug fixes, plugin updates.

Ask:

- "What happens when something breaks after launch?"
- "Do you offer a maintenance retainer?"
- "Can I update the site myself, and will you train me to do it?"

Agencies that disappear after launch leave you stuck with problems you cannot fix and a site you cannot update.

## 8. Compare Proposals Properly {#compare}

When comparing quotes from different agencies, make sure you are comparing the same scope. A UGX 1.35M quote that includes everything is better value than an UGX 800K quote that charges separately for hosting, SSL and mobile optimisation.

## Red Flags to Avoid {#red-flags}

<div class="warn">
<strong>⚠️ Walk away if you see any of these:</strong>

- No written contract or scope of work before payment
- No portfolio of live websites (only mockups or screenshots)
- Cannot explain how they will get your site on Google
- Promises first-page Google ranking "within 2 weeks" (this is not how SEO works)
- Domain will be registered in their name, not yours
- No clarity on who owns the code after the project
- Asks for 100% payment upfront before any work begins
- No plan for what happens after launch
</div>

## Questions to Ask Before Signing {#questions}

<div class="checklist">
<h4>✅ Pre-Signing Checklist</h4>

- Can I see 5 live websites you have built recently?
- What is your exact process from brief to launch?
- What is included in the quote — domain, hosting, SSL, SEO, content upload?
- Who owns the domain and hosting account after launch?
- How many revision rounds are included?
- How do I reach you if something breaks after the site goes live?
- Can I update the site myself, and will you train me?
- What is the timeline from start to launch?

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faqs}

> "How do I know if an agency is trustworthy?"

Ask for references — not testimonials on their own website, but actual client contact details you can reach out to independently. A confident, legitimate agency will provide these without hesitation.

> "Is cheaper always worse?"

Not necessarily. A freelancer charging UGX 800K who has a strong portfolio, clear process and good communication can be excellent value. An agency charging UGX 3M with vague answers and no live portfolio examples can be a waste of money. Evaluate on evidence, not price.

> "What is a reasonable timeline for a business website in Uganda?"

A standard 6 to 10 page business website should take 2 to 4 weeks from the discovery call to launch, assuming you provide your content promptly. Longer than 6 weeks for a basic site is a warning sign.

> "Should I hire a freelancer or an agency?"

Freelancers are fine for basic websites if they have a strong portfolio. An agency is better when you need ongoing support, multiple services (design + SEO + ads) and accountability. See our [website cost guide](/blog/how-much-does-a-website-cost-in-uganda/) for a comparison.

> "How do I avoid getting locked in to one supplier?"

Insist on owning your domain, hosting account and all the code. Get this in writing before the project starts. A reputable agency will have no problem with this — it is a standard requirement.
