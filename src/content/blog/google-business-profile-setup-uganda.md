Your Google Business Profile is the single most important free tool available to any Uganda business owner. It is what puts your business on Google Maps, in the local pack at the top of search results and in front of customers who are actively looking for what you sell.

This guide walks you through setting it up, verifying it and optimising it to rank above your competitors in Kampala and across Uganda.

## What is a Google Business Profile?

A Google Business Profile (previously called Google My Business) is a free listing that appears when someone searches for your business or your type of business on Google Search and Google Maps.

When someone searches "restaurant Ntinda" or "accountant Kampala," the map with three business listings that appears at the top of results is the **local pack**. Every business in that local pack has a verified, optimised Google Business Profile.

Without a profile, you are invisible in those results.

## Before You Start

You will need:
- A Google account (Gmail)
- Your business name, address and phone number (must be consistent with what's on your website)
- Your business category
- A physical address or service area in Uganda
- A working phone number to receive verification

## Step 1: Create Your Profile

1. Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com) and sign in with your Google account
2. Click "Manage now" or "Add your business"
3. Type your business name — if Google suggests an existing listing, claim it rather than creating a duplicate
4. Select your business category — choose carefully, this is the most important field (more on this below)
5. Choose whether customers visit your location or you go to them:
   - If customers come to you: enter your full address
   - If you go to customers: enter your service area (Kampala, Wakiso, etc.)
6. Add your phone number and website URL
7. Click "Finish"

## Step 2: Verify Your Business

Google requires verification to confirm you actually own or operate the business. In Uganda, the most common verification methods are:

**Postcard by mail** — Google sends a postcard with a PIN to your address. This takes 1–2 weeks. Enter the PIN in your profile to complete verification.

**Phone verification** — available for some business types. Google calls your number with a PIN.

**Email verification** — available for some accounts.

**Video verification** — Google may ask you to record a short video showing your business location, signage and equipment.

Complete verification as quickly as possible — an unverified profile has limited visibility.

## Step 3: Complete Every Section

Once verified, fill out every section of your profile. Incomplete profiles rank lower than complete ones.

### Business Name
Use your exact legal business name. Do not add keywords to your name (e.g., "KTM Agency Web Design Kampala") — Google penalises this practice.

### Category
Your primary category is the most influential field in your profile. Choose the most specific category that accurately describes your main business:

- Web design agency → "Web Design Company"
- Restaurant → "Restaurant" (not "Food Service")
- Clinic → "Medical Clinic"
- Hotel → "Hotel"

You can add additional secondary categories for other services you offer.

### Description
Write a 200–300 word description that:
- Mentions your primary service and location in the first sentence
- Includes your key services naturally
- Explains what makes you different
- Does NOT include links or promotional claims like "best in Uganda"

Example opening: *"KTM Agency is a web design and digital marketing company based in Kampala, Uganda. We help businesses across Uganda build professional websites, rank higher on Google and run effective advertising campaigns on Google Ads and Facebook."*

### Services
List every specific service you offer. This helps Google match your profile to more relevant searches.

### Opening Hours
Add accurate hours. If you operate on WhatsApp outside those hours, mention it in your description. Update hours for public holidays.

### Photos
Photos are one of the most impactful elements of your profile. Businesses with more photos get significantly more views and clicks.

Upload at minimum:
- **Logo** — your brand mark
- **Cover photo** — a wide image of your business or work
- **Interior photos** — if you have a physical location
- **Team photos** — puts a face to the business
- **Work samples** — examples of your products or services
- **Exterior** — so customers can find you

Aim for 15+ photos. Add new ones monthly.

### Products and Services Menu
For retail businesses and restaurants, add your products or menu items with photos and prices. This increases your visibility in product search results.

## Step 4: Get Your First Reviews

Reviews are the second most important ranking factor in local SEO after profile completeness.

**How to get your first 10 reviews:**

1. Go to your Google Business Profile dashboard
2. Click "Get more reviews" and copy the direct review link
3. Send this link via WhatsApp to your most satisfied customers with a short message: *"We'd really appreciate if you could leave us a Google review — it only takes 2 minutes and helps us a lot. Here's the link: [link]"*
4. Follow up once if they don't respond within a week

Never offer incentives for reviews — this violates Google's policies. Just ask directly and make it easy.

**Respond to every review:**
- Thank positive reviewers personally
- Respond professionally to negative reviews — acknowledge the issue and offer to resolve it

Businesses that respond to reviews rank higher than those that don't.

## Step 5: Post Regular Updates

Google Business Profile allows you to post updates, offers, events and news. Posting at least once a week signals to Google that your business is active.

Ideas for posts:
- New service or product announcements
- Special offers or promotions
- Behind-the-scenes photos
- Tips relevant to your customers
- Answers to common customer questions

Each post can include a photo, text and a call-to-action button (call, visit website, book now).

## Step 6: Add Questions and Answers

The Q&A section of your profile often goes unnoticed. You can add your own questions and answer them — this is a powerful way to include keywords and provide information customers commonly ask for.

Good Q&A examples:
- Q: "Do you offer website maintenance?" A: "Yes, we offer monthly maintenance packages for all websites we build..."
- Q: "What areas in Uganda do you serve?" A: "We serve businesses across Uganda including Kampala, Entebbe, Jinja and Mbarara..."

## Monitoring Your Profile

Check your profile insights monthly in the Google Business Profile dashboard. You will see:

- How many times your profile was viewed
- How customers found you (direct search vs discovery)
- What actions they took (called, visited website, requested directions)

Use this data to identify what is working and what needs improvement.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Keyword stuffing in business name** — Google will suspend your profile
- **Wrong category** — you'll miss out on relevant searches
- **No photos** — profiles with photos get 40%+ more requests for directions
- **Not responding to reviews** — hurts both rankings and reputation
- **Inconsistent NAP** — different phone number or address vs your website confuses Google
