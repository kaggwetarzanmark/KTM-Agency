## What is Google Business Profile? {#what-is-gbp}

Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is a free tool from Google that controls how your business appears on Google Search and Google Maps.

When someone in Kampala searches "dentist in Ntinda," "hotel near Entebbe airport," or "hardware shop Nakawa," Google shows a box of three businesses with a map at the top of the results. This is the **local pack**, the most valuable real estate on Google Search. Every business in that box has a verified, optimised Google Business Profile.

Your profile shows:
- Business name, address and phone number
- Opening hours
- Photos and videos
- Star ratings and customer reviews
- Website link and directions
- Products, services and prices
- Posts and updates

<div class="tip">
<strong>💡 Expert Tip from KTM Agency:</strong> Your Google Business Profile is often the very first interaction a potential customer has with your brand, before your website, before social media, before they call you. Treat it like your most important digital asset.

## How to Create Your Profile (Step-by-Step) {#create}

**Step 1, Go to Google Business Profile**

Visit [business.google.com](https://business.google.com) and sign in with a Google account. Use a business Gmail account, not your personal one.

**Step 2, Enter your business name**

Type your real, legal business name exactly as it appears on your signage and documents. Do not stuff keywords like "Best Clinic Kampala Affordable." Google penalises this and it looks unprofessional to customers.

**Step 3, Choose your business category**

This is the most important field in your entire profile, it controls which searches you appear for. Choose the category that most precisely describes your main business. We cover this in detail in Section 7.

**Step 4, Add your location**

- If customers visit you: enter your full street address including the area (e.g., Ntinda, Kampala)
- If you go to customers: skip the address and set a service area instead

**Step 5, Add contact details**

Add your Ugandan phone number and your website URL. If you do not have a website yet, this is the most important reason to get one, a Business Profile without a website link loses significant ranking power and credibility.

**Step 6, Set your business hours**

Be accurate. A customer who arrives at a closed business will leave a negative review. Update hours for public holidays.

**Step 7, Verify your business** (see next section)

**Step 8, Complete every remaining section**

Description, photos, services, attributes, products, leave nothing blank.

## How to Verify Your Business {#verify}

Verification confirms to Google that you are a legitimate operator at the location you claim. Without it, your profile will not appear in Search or Maps results.

Methods available in Uganda:

| Method | How it works | Typical time |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Video verification | Record a short video showing your storefront, signage and surroundings | 1 to 5 days |
| Phone or SMS | Google sends a code to your registered number | Instant |
| Email | Verification code sent to your business email | Minutes |
| Postcard by mail | Google posts a physical card with a PIN (rare in Uganda now) | 2 to 4 weeks |

<div class="tip">
<strong>💡 Expert Tip:</strong> Video verification has become the most common method for Uganda businesses. Prepare your business signage, the exterior of your premises and any trading documents before recording. Google wants to see that your business is real and at the address you claimed.
</div>

<div class="warn">
<strong>⚠️ Warning:</strong> Never pay a third party who claims they can "instantly verify" your profile outside Google's official process. This violates Google's terms and can result in permanent suspension.
</div>

## How to Optimise Your Profile to Rank Higher {#optimise}

Creating a profile is step one. Optimising it is what makes you visible above your competitors. Most Uganda businesses set up a profile once and never touch it again, this is your opportunity to pull ahead.

A fully optimised profile includes:

- 100% complete business information
- The right primary and secondary categories
- A keyword-rich, naturally written business description
- At least 15 photos updated regularly
- Actively managed reviews with responses
- Weekly Google Posts
- Listed products and services with prices

## Choosing the Right Business Category {#categories}

Your primary category is one of the strongest ranking signals Google uses. Get it wrong and you will be invisible for the searches that matter most.

Rules for choosing:
- Choose the category that most precisely describes your core business
- Add secondary categories for other services you genuinely offer
- Avoid broad categories just because they sound bigger, precision beats guesswork
- Re-check your categories every few months; Google adds new specific categories regularly

Examples for common Uganda business types:

| Business | Primary category | Secondary categories |
|---------|-----------------|---------------------|
| Restaurant in Kampala | Restaurant | Catering service, Bar |
| Boutique hotel | Hotel | Resort hotel, Event venue |
| Private clinic | Medical clinic | Doctor, Pharmacy |
| Primary or secondary school | Primary school / Secondary school | Boarding school, Tutoring service |
| Law firm | Law firm | Legal services, Notary public |
| Real estate agency | Real estate agency | Property management company |
| Web design agency | Web design company | SEO agency, Digital marketing agency |

<div class="tip">
<strong>💡 Expert Tip:</strong> Check which category your top-ranking competitors are using by searching for your service in Google Maps and clicking on competitors' profiles. If several high-ranking businesses use a specific category, that is a strong signal it is the right one for your market.

## Writing an SEO-Friendly Description {#description}

Your business description (up to 750 characters) speaks to both Google's algorithm and human readers. Most Uganda businesses write something generic like "We provide quality services." This is a wasted opportunity.

**Formula for a strong description:**

1. Open with what you do and where you are located
2. Mention your core services naturally (include your main keyword once or twice)
3. Highlight what makes you different from competitors
4. End with a soft call-to-action

**Example for a Kampala web design agency:**

*"KTM Agency is a web design and digital marketing company based in Kampala, Uganda. We build fast, SEO-ready websites for businesses across Uganda, manage Google Ads and Facebook Ads campaigns, and provide local SEO services that help Ugandan businesses rank on Google. Trusted by businesses in Kampala, Entebbe, Jinja and Mbarara. Get a free quote in 24 hours."*

<div class="warn">
<strong>⚠️ Warning:</strong> Never keyword-stuff your description ("Best Web Design Kampala Cheap Web Design Uganda Web Developer Near Me"). Google detects and penalises this, and it reads as spam to real customers.
</div>

What to upload:

| Photo type | Purpose | Tips |
|-----------|---------|------|

## Managing Reviews Like a Pro {#reviews}

Reviews are the second most important ranking factor in Google Maps after profile completeness. Businesses with more positive reviews consistently outrank those with fewer, even when other factors are similar.

**How to get your first 10 reviews:**

1. Copy your direct review link from the Google Business Profile dashboard (Share button → Copy link)
2. Send it via WhatsApp to your 10 most satisfied customers with a simple message: *"We'd really appreciate a Google review, it only takes 2 minutes and helps us a lot. Here's the link: [paste link]"*
3. Follow up once after one week if they have not responded

**How to respond to reviews:**

| Review type | Response approach |
|------------|------------------|
| 5-star positive | Thank them personally, mention a specific detail from their review, invite them back |
| 3 to 4 star | Thank them, acknowledge the specific issue they raised, offer to resolve it |
| 1 to 2 star | Stay professional, acknowledge their experience, offer direct contact to resolve it offline |
| Fake or unfair | Flag it for Google removal, respond calmly without arguing |

<div class="warn">
<strong>⚠️ Warning:</strong> Never offer money, discounts or free services in exchange for reviews. Google detects incentivised reviews and can suspend your profile. Simply ask satisfied customers genuinely and make the process easy.
</div>

## Google Posts and Updates {#posts}

Google Posts let you publish updates, offers, events and news directly on your profile. Posting at least once a week signals to Google that your business is active, a ranking factor that most Uganda businesses completely ignore.

Post ideas for Uganda businesses:

- New service or product launch
- Seasonal offers or promotions
- Behind-the-scenes photos of your team
- Tips relevant to your customers
- Client success stories (with permission)
- Public holiday hours announcements

Each post can include a photo, up to 1,500 words of text and a call-to-action button (Call, Visit website, Book, Order online).

<div class="tip">
<strong>💡 Expert Tip:</strong> Posts expire after 7 days by default but are still visible on your profile. Build a simple habit: every Monday morning, publish one new post. This alone puts you ahead of 90% of Uganda businesses on Google Maps.
</div>

## Google Maps Ranking Factors {#ranking}

Google uses three core factors to decide which businesses appear in the local pack:

**1. Relevance**, How closely your profile matches what the user searched for. This is determined by your category, your description, your services list and the keywords in your reviews.

**2. Distance**, How far your business is from the user's location or the location they specified in the search. You cannot change your physical location, but you can expand your service area.

**3. Prominence**, How well-known and trusted your business is. This is determined by the number and quality of your reviews, how many websites link to you, how complete your profile is and how active you are (posts, photos, responses).

<div class="stat">
<strong>📌 Key insight:</strong> Distance is the only factor you cannot directly influence. Relevance and Prominence are both within your control, which means consistent profile management directly translates to higher rankings.
</div>

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faqs}

**How long does it take for my Google Business Profile to appear in results?**

After verification, most profiles appear in Google Maps within 3 to 7 days. For the profile to rank well in the local pack, consistent optimisation over 4 to 8 weeks is typically needed.

**Can I have a Google Business Profile without a website?**

Yes, but your ranking and conversion rate will be significantly lower. A Business Profile without a website link loses trust signals and ranking power. A basic professional website in Uganda starts from UGX 720,000, the return on investment is fast.

**What if my business address is in a residential area?**

You can hide your exact address and show only your service area. This is common for home-based businesses. You still need to enter your real address during verification, Google just will not display it publicly.

**How do I deal with a competitor who has fake reviews?**

Report each suspicious review to Google using the flag option. Document the reviews. If the issue is serious, submit a Business Redressal Complaint through Google's support channel. Focus on getting your own genuine reviews, volume and recency matter more than any individual fake review.

**Does having more reviews automatically make me rank higher?**

Reviews are one of several factors. A profile with 50 genuine reviews and complete information will typically outrank a profile with 200 reviews but an incomplete profile and no posts. All factors work together.
